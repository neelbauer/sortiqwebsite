What is SortIQ

SortIQ's personality is more closer in proximity to developer than recruiter becasue we are developers who recruit. Hiring teams and candidadates can expect a fast, fluid conversations, and a candidate funnel that elevates the sort of your people you need prefer so you can scale faster, and on the right terms so they'll be motivated to stick, be a part of your team, and on the same mission. While we can't overcome the odds of a compensation advantage if you are going up against a better funded team, we can help you connect with people who would rather work with you and experience suggests our approach is better than throwing more money at the problem.

Why Try SortIQ

Our market tested research and community engagement methodology using software tools and both sourcing and recruiting techniques developed internally over 19 years. We belive recruiting is a get out of your own way problem so we pull out all the stops by tearing down the medieval castle style fortifications, drain the moat, update the screening and interview guantlet, pick up the phone and talk to candidates, and focus on an enticing conversation focused on helping canddiates see themselves as a part of a team they want to be a part of. Not every candidate is a hire, but they have friends and treat people well which lends itself to referrals. Our biggest issue is that the sort of people we recruit stick around for a really long time so there isn't as much demand for our services later on. That hasn't stopped CEO's who know our work from calling us back and telling their friends about us years and even decades after they we last helped them. In many cases, our clients utilize our consulting services beyond recruiting because we don't just recruit...we build software, offer community engagement, and have a growing frontier talent and AI focused practice area for companies looking to embrace and better utilize frontier models in their software engineering and business efforts.
